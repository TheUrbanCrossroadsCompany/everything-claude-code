02 — The differentiator

Proteins, not ctDNA.

20/20 measures tumor proteins instead of circulating tumor DNA. The signal shows up earlier, and every report carries the actual biomarker values.

The ctDNA approach

Detects fragments of circulating tumor DNA shed into the bloodstream.

The 20/20 approach

Measures tumor proteins directly. Detection comes earlier, and each report shows the actual biomarker values.
