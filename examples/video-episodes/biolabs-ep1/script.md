# Episode 1 — Narration Script

Read-aloud script for the cloned voice. Each block maps 1:1 to a scene in `storyboard.json`
(the `vo` field). Durations are estimates until Stage 2 measures the rendered audio.

**Voice rules:** direct, compressed, concrete. Numbers and mechanisms over adjectives. No
"excited to share", no bait questions, no exclamation points, no hype adjectives.

**Fact-lock:** every number below is from the source brief. Nothing invented. 2028 Medicare
and the fall FDA meeting are targets/plans, not accomplished facts.

---

## Scene 1 — Hero (ink) · ~9s

This is the monthly investor recap for 20/20 BioLabs, a clinical lab in Gaithersburg,
Maryland, building early cancer detection from tumor proteins, plus a new longevity test on
inflammatory biomarkers. Your speakers: CEO Jonathan Cohen and Director of Sales David Lees.

## Scene 2 — The business today (paper) · ~15s

Start with the business. Revenue runs between two-and-a-half and three million dollars a
year, and it is growing forty to fifty percent. Twenty-nine new accounts landed in the
second quarter. The step change ahead is Medicare reimbursement, targeted for twenty
twenty-eight.

## Scene 3 — Proteins, not ctDNA (paper) · ~16s

The differentiator: 20/20 measures tumor proteins, not circulating tumor DNA. The signal
shows up earlier, and every report carries the actual biomarker values, so repeat tests plot
a trajectory over time. Groups disappointed with ctDNA-based tests are switching.

## Scene 4 — Best quarter (paper) · ~16s

Q2 was the best cancer-testing quarter in company history. The twenty-nine new accounts came
from five categories: fire departments, occupational health groups, preventive health
companies, nonprofits, and physician offices. The Maryland state firefighter screening
program is live; Vermont is next.

## Scene 5 — Giant Foods pilot (paper, teal) · ~13s

On the longevity side, a pilot with Giant Foods. Employees test first, pairing inflammatory
biomarkers with dietary data. If it works, shelf placement could follow. An update is due at
the October webinar.

## Scene 6 — East Asia (ink) · ~17s

The CEO's East Asia trip: a region with a strong prevention culture. He reconnected with
collaborators, built new relationships, and gathered real-world data for FDA submissions and
Medicare coverage, while scouting acquisitions. The plan: a royalty pool putting five percent
of post-Medicare revenue back to the partner groups that contribute data.

## Scene 7 — What happens next (paper) · ~14s

What is next, in four milestones. September's webinar covers the East Asia trip in detail.
October brings Giant Foods pilot results. The first formal FDA meeting is planned for the
fall. And Medicare reimbursement is the twenty twenty-eight target.

## Scene 8 — Close (ink) · ~14s

The shape of the bet: biopharma upside, with device-grade downside protection. For investor
questions or M&A leads, reach out to the 20/20 BioLabs team. Webinar attendees got a
limited-time code, Web20. This is an informational recap, not an offer to sell securities;
targets are goals, not guarantees.
